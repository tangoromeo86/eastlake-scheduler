🏟 My Fields

Add Field

Drag the pin (or click the map) to the exact center of the field — the address only gets you close, and won't tell fields apart when a park has several.

Prefer to do it by hand?
  1. Open Google Maps and find the field.
  2. Right-click the exact spot (press and hold on a phone).
  3. Click the numbers at the top of the menu that pops up — something like 41.535017, -81.461610. That copies them.
  4. Paste them into the Coordinates box above.
